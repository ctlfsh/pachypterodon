The Difference Between Dinosaurs, Prehistoric Reptiles, and Prehistoric Marine Reptiles

A wild trip through prehistoric time — who ruled the land, the sky, and the deep blue sea!

🦕 Dinosaurs 🦅 Prehistoric Reptiles 🌊 Marine Reptiles

🔍 Meet the Groups!

Example

Pachycephalosaurus!

Dinosaurs ruled the land for over 160 million years! What sets them apart? Their legs go straight under their bodies — giving them an upright, powerful stance. The Pachycephalosaurus is famous for its domed skull, up to 10 inches thick — it could headbutt rivals like a living battering ram!

🌿 Land Rulers
Example

Quetzalcoatlus!

Flying reptiles called pterosaurs ruled the skies — but they were NOT dinosaurs! They were close cousins. The Quetzalcoatlus is the largest flying animal ever known, with a wingspan up to 36 feet — as wide as a small airplane — and stood as tall as a giraffe on the ground!

☁️ Sky Flyers
Example

Mosasaurus!

While dinosaurs ruled the land, sea monsters ruled the oceans! Marine reptiles were NOT dinosaurs either — they were reptiles that went back to the sea. The Mosasaurus had a jaw like a snake and a powerful tail that could propel it up to 50 feet long through the deep!
